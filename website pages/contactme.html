About the project

NavalShips Wikia

NavalShips Wikia is a collaborative project documenting naval ships from the past and present. It brings together historical summaries, technical specifications, fleet context, and ship-class information in one accessible reference.

The wiki currently focuses mainly on ships from the World War II era, while continuing to grow toward broader coverage of naval history and technology.
